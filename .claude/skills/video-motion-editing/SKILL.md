# Video Motion Editing — أنواع حركات المونتاج

Source: "أنواع حركات المونتاج" by Majed Al-Zaabi (ماجد الزعابي). Condensed into an operational playbook.

## Core idea

A number alone doesn't land; motion makes the viewer **feel** it. Saying "60% of people" is heard. Showing the 60 built from tiny walking people is understood and remembered.

Motion does three jobs — every move must serve at least one:

| Job | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hook** — تمسك العين | The first 3 seconds decide continue vs. scroll. |
| **Explain** — تشرح | Show the idea instead of saying it: ratio, comparison, journey. |
| **Stick** — تبقى | A moving image stays in memory longer than speech. |

Motion is an idea before it is software. If you can't state what a move explains or emphasizes, cut it.

## 1. Camera moves — حركات الكاميرا

| Move | Arabic | What it does | Try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Push In** | دفع للأمام | Slow approach; focuses attention on an important moment. | Slow push on the number *before* you say it. |
| **Pull Out** | سحب للخلف | Reveals the bigger picture; great for surprise. | Pull from a city out to the whole globe. |
| **Pan** | بانوراما | Horizontal rotation in place; shows a space or links two elements. | Pan tower-to-tower in one shot. |
| **Tracking Shot** | تتبع | Camera moves with the subject; viewer feels *with* them. | Track the car entering the desert road. |
| **Orbit** | مدار | Circles the subject; gives size and presence. | Orbit the product/building in the first 2 s. |
| **FPV Drone** | منظور الطيار | Fast flight between/around elements; viewer *is* the drone. | Fly between city towers with a fast bank. |

**Rule:** one camera move per shot. Never push + rotate + pan at once.

## 2. Transitions — الانتقالات

Goal: move between shots without the viewer feeling the cut.

1. **Match Cut** — قطع مطابق: two shots matching in shape or motion (car wheel → moon).
2. **Whip Pan** — مسح سريع: a fast camera whip hides the cut; high rhythm for Reels.
3. **Zoom Through** — تكبير عابر: enter through a window/screen, exit into a new scene.
4. **Jump Cut** — قطع قفزي: remove silences from speech. Most-used in talking-head content.
5. **Split Screen** — تقسيم الشاشة: simultaneous shots for comparison or "happening at once".
6. **Shape Morph** — تحوّل بالشكل: one element becomes another (number → ball → map).

**Quick test:** if the viewer notices the transition more than the shot, simplify it.

## 3. Kinetic text — النص المتحرك

The word appears at its moment, so it is read *and* heard.

| Technique | Arabic | Use | Try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kinetic Typography** | نص مع الكلام | Words move on the audio's timing, scale up on emphasis. | Enlarge only the key word in the sentence. |
| **Text Behind Subject** | نص خلف الشخص | Big title behind the person → depth, magazine-cover look. | First shot of the reel with a title behind you. |
| **Word by Word** | كلمة بكلمة | One word at a time; forces the viewer to keep watching. | Use it in the hook, first 3 s. |
| **Number Counter** | عدّاد أرقام | Counts from 0 to value; viewer feels the magnitude. | Counter to 1500 with accelerating speed. |
| **Glitch** | تشويش رقمي | Small shake/corruption; tech and surprises. | Half a second on the shock shot only. |
| **Typewriter** | كتابة تدريجية | Letters typed in; calm and suspense. | Type a question, answer it in the next shot. |

**Arabic text rule:** pick a bold, clear typeface and keep connected letters intact. **Never animate Arabic letters individually** — it breaks the joins. Animate by word or line.

## 4. Motion graphics & infographics — الموشن قرافيك والانفو قرافيك

- **Motion Graphics** — icons, shapes, drawings moving over or without footage. Best for: explaining an abstract idea, ads.
- **Infographic Video** — animated numbers and charts that turn a statistic into a story. Best for: ratio, comparison, ranking.

Three rules for good explanation:
1. **One element moves at a time.** If everything moves, nothing is noticed.
2. **Motion follows the voice.** An element appears exactly on the word that names it.
3. **Leave a second to read.** After every number or title, let the eye catch up.

**Color:** two main colors + one accent. Never more than three in a scene.

## 5. Full editing styles — ستايلات المونتاج الكاملة

A style is the whole video's language, not one move.

| Style | Arabic | Character | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper Cutout / Collage** | قصاصات | Photos and paper scraps, choppy motion, cut edges. | Story, memories, history. |
| **Surreal Editing** | سريالي | Unreal scenes, strange morphs that break logic. | Shock hook, idea ad. |
| **Documentary** | وثائقي | B-roll + voiceover + calm music. | Trips, safari, true stories. |
| **Fast Cuts** | إيقاع سريع | Short cuts on the beat, high energy. | Reels, TikTok. |
| **Cinematic** | سينمائي | Deliberate color, slow motion, detail. | Nature, architecture, ads. |
| **Stop Motion** | إيقاف الحركة | Frame-by-frame, choppy handmade feel. | Products, playful ideas. |

**Rule:** one style per video. Mixing styles destroys the work's identity.

## 6. Animating numbers & percentages — حركات الأرقام والنسب

General principle: turn the number into a visual element that explains its meaning — don't just type it on screen.

Patterns (from the source, generalized):
1. **Composite figure** — "60% of people": the digits are built from tiny people walking into place along the strokes. Smaller icons = stronger sense of count.
2. **Fill level** — "75% of Earth is water": a sphere fills to a level computed from area, with two waves at different speeds.
3. **Same-scale comparison** — "Tallest three towers in the Gulf": side by side at true scale, a ruler shows the meter difference *before* the numbers are spoken.
4. **Journey on a map** — plane then car across a borderless globe (e.g. Doha → Nairobi → Maasai Mara).
5. **2D → 3D** — a flat line drawing inflates into a 3D model, then an FPV fly-through.

## How to find ideas — فكّك بدل ما تقلّد

Deconstruct any video you like into **six layers**:

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| **Rhythm** — الإيقاع | Where does pacing speed up / slow down? |
| **Transitions** — الانتقالات | How does each shot hand off? |
| **Type** — الخطوط | Font, size, how many words per shot? |
| **Color** — الألوان | Base palette + accent? |
| **Effects** — المؤثرات | Glitch, blur, light leaks, grain? |
| **Sound** — الصوت | SFX on cuts, music drops, VO timing? |

Take **one or two layers** and add them to your own style — that is how a personal style forms.

Worked example — "borderless journey" reel:
- Rhythm: slow during flight, faster on arrival.
- Transition: zoom-through from globe to ground.
- Type: wide and clear, one word per shot.
- Color: calm ground, one accent color for the route.

## Applying this skill

When asked to plan or critique an edit:
1. Name the **job** of the video's first 3 s (hook) and pick one hook technique (word-by-word, text behind subject, push-in on a number, shock glitch).
2. Pick **one style** for the whole video (§5).
3. For every statistic, propose a **number visual** (§6) instead of on-screen text.
4. Output a **shot list**: `time | shot | camera move | transition out | text on screen | SFX`. One camera move per shot.
5. Run the checks: one element moving at a time · motion on the spoken word · 1 s read time after numbers · ≤ 3 colors · no per-letter Arabic animation · transition never louder than the shot.

When building in **Remotion**, map techniques to primitives: push-in/pull-out = `interpolate(frame, …)` on `scale`; counter = interpolated value with `Math.round` and an ease-in curve for "accelerating" feel; word-by-word / kinetic type = per-word `Sequence` timed to captions (Arabic: per word, never per glyph); split screen = flex layout of `OffthreadVideo`; transitions = `@remotion/transitions`. Load the Remotion skills for API details.
