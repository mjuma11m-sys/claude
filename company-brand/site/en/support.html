Support

Common questions about our apps. Not answered here? Write to us.

Is Hayati on the App Store or Google Play?

Not yet. Hayati is in development (beta). Join the waitlist on the Hayati page and we will email you once when it is out.

Do I need an account?

No. The main features work on your device without an account. An account is optional, for backup and sync between your devices.

Where is my data stored?

On your device first. If you create an account, a copy is stored on Supabase in the Frankfurt region (Germany), and each account can only see its own data.

How do I delete my data?

In Hayati: Settings → “Delete all my data”. It erases the device copy and, if you have an account, the account copy too. For data from this website’s forms, contact us through the form.

What is the status of Idarati?

Beta, in development. You can try it on the web from the Idarati page.

I found a problem or have an idea

Write to us through the contact form: the app name, your device, and exactly what happened. We aim to reply within 3 working days.
