Tester agreement

Draft for review, not binding legal advice.

  1. What you test: a beta of Hayati, in development, through TestFlight (iPhone) or Google Play closed testing (Android). It may have bugs, and features may change.
  2. How long: Google requires 14 consecutive days on Android, so please keep the app installed that long if you can. Not mandatory.
  3. No warranty: the app is provided as is. Don’t rely on it alone for money decisions, and keep a backup.
  4. Your data: we only need your email to add you to the test. Data inside the app stays on your device unless you create an account. We never ask for screenshots with your real numbers.
  5. Your feedback: we may use your ideas and notes to improve the app, without payment, and without naming you unless you agree.
  6. Confidentiality: please don’t publish screenshots or details of unannounced features. Anything on the website or in the store is not confidential.
  7. Leaving: any time, no reason needed. Delete the app, and contact us through the form if you want your email removed.
  8. Age and law: 18 or older; the laws of the Kingdom of Bahrain apply.
