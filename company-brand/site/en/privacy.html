Privacy policy

This policy covers the ${HOST} website only. Each of our apps (such as Hayati) has its own policy inside the app.

When you browse

There are no cookies, ads or third-party analytics. Fonts and images are served from this domain. We count page opens (only the path, page language and day, with no IP address or identifier) to learn which pages are read, and we do not count you if your browser sends Do Not Track. Our host (Vercel) logs standard technical data for each visit (IP address, browser, time) to run and protect the site, under its own policy.

When you fill in a form

  • What we store: your email, your name and message if you wrote them, which app you signed up for, the page language, and the time.
  • Why: to reply to you, or to email you once when the app is ready. We do not add you to any other mailing list.
  • Where: a Supabase database in the Frankfurt region (Germany), outside Bahrain. We ask for your consent to this before you send.
  • Who sees it: only WADQA’s founder. We do not publish or sell it.
  • How long: 24 months at most, then it is deleted.

Your rights

Under Bahrain’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 30 of 2018), you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent by emailing wadqa.studio.bh@gmail.com.
